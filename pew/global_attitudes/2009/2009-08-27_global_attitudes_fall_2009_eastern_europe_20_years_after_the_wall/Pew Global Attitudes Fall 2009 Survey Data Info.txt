PEW RESEARCH CENTER - Global Attitudes Project
Fall 2009 14 Nation Survey 


It is important to read this information before using the Fall 2009 Pew Global Attitudes Survey dataset. 

 
1.	For the general population:

	a.	The weight that must be used when looking to analyze the attitudes and opinions of the general populations are:
		
		General population variable name:  Weight
			


2.	In Germany, there was an oversample of east Germans bringing the total number of east German respondents  to 800. 
		
		To analyze east Germans in Germany:

		Filter for east Germans: select if Q74Ger = 2. 
		
		Weight by weight for this analysis. 


3.	The topline provides results for the general populations surveyed. 


4. 	For Germany, the topline provides results for the general population as well as for east and west Germans for 2010 as well as 1991.


5.	The topline also provides full question wording for all questions included in the survey as well as footnotes concerning variations in question wording for trend data from previous surveys.
