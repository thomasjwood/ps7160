PEW RESEARCH CENTER FOR THE PEOPLE & THE PRESS
EARLY OCTOBER CALLBACK STUDY
October 3-5, 2008
N=820

The 820 interviews were conducted among a population of 1,505 adults previously interviewed by Pew from September 27-29.   The survey included callbacks to both landline and cell phones.

In order to assure comparability of the recontacted cases with the original sample, the data were also weighted to match the distribution of the Sept. 27-29 sample results on key political questions, including leaned party affiliation, whether Sarah Palin is qualified to be president and whether the government plan to secure the markets is a good or bad thing to do. This step helps to minimize bias that could occur because certain types of respondents may have been easier or harder to re-interview. The weighting used an iterative technique that simultaneously balances the distributions of all weighting parameters. 

The callback study included 13 questions, labeled rthought through qr14 in this dataset.  All other variables in this dataset (including interview date and interviewer characteristics) are from the original Sept 27-29 interviews with these respondents. 

For information on created variables such as swing, Q5horse, Battle, LVS, see the readme file for the Sept 27-29 survey.

Only cases that were recontacted Oct 3-5 are included in this dataset. For full results from the original 1,505 interviews, download the full Sept 27-29 dataset.
