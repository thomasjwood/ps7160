November 2008 Post-Election Re-Interview Survey
Nov. 6-9, 2008
1,556 total respondents
1,500 voters (on whom all analysis was conducted and on whom the topline is based).
THe 1,500 includes 1,125 landline interviews and 375 cell-phone interviews. Of the cell-phone respondents, 114 did not have landlines.

Conducted by Princeton Survey Research Associates International (PSRA), calling back registered voters
from the mid-October 2008 poll conducted by and Abt SRBI.

The variable that should be used to weight this recontact survey is called WEIGHT. The survey was weighted for standard demographics, but also to reflect the margin of the election results (an approximate 7-point advantage for Obama) to improve comparability of the recontacted cases with the 2008 election results.

Questions from the mid-October interview that are included in this dataset have a "p" before their 
question numbers. These are included to analyze the reinterview data only.  Use the full mid-October dataset for any analyses of these variables. 


NOTE: First is a new form of the first-time-voter question that replcaes the Pfirs1 and Pfirs2 variables from October.

Letter grades represent averages calculated on a standard 4.0 scale (i.e., A=4.0, B=3.0, etc.), based on those who could assign grades.
All averages are rounded up to the next highest grade (e.g., a 2.70 is considered a B— a 2.67 is a B-).
