PEW RESEARCH CENTER FOR THE PEOPLE & THE PRESS
OCTOBER 2011 KNOWLEDGE SURVEY
SEPTEBMER 30-OCTOBER 11, 2011 
TOTAL N=1,168

***************************************************************************************************************************

The October Knowledge Survey was conducted by Knowledge Networks among their nationally representative online panel. 
The October Knowledge Survey included both visual and verbal questions. For more details on the survey 
see the About the Survey page of the report: http://www.people-press.org/2011/11/07/what-the-public-knows-in-words-and-pictures/2/#about-the-survey. 

The data are weighted; the WEIGHT variable should be used for analysis of this data.

***************************************************************************************************************************

To protect the privacy of respondents, county of residence and zip code have been removed from the data file.

***************************************************************************************************************************

A seaprate dataset with the order and timing variables is provided here. This dataset can be merged with the main dataset 
by matching on the caseid.
